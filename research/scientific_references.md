# Scientific References & Literature

Key peer-reviewed literature, NASA technical reports, and clinical guidelines informing the SPACECARE anomaly detection thresholds and mitigation recommendations.

---

## 1. Cardiovascular Health & Microgravity Deconditioning
- **Hughson, R. L., et al. (2016)**. *Cardiovascular regulation during long-duration spaceflights to the International Space Station.* Journal of Applied Physiology, 121(1), 177-184.
  - Documents post-flight orthostatic intolerance, arterial stiffening, and fluid shift dynamics.
- **Norsk, P. (2014)**. *Blood pressure regulation IV: adaptive responses to weightlessness.* European Journal of Applied Physiology, 114(3), 481-497.

## 2. Musculoskeletal Health & Bone Mineral Density Loss
- **LeBlanc, A., et al. (2007)**. *Bisphosphonates as a countermeasure to decrease bone loss during long-duration space flight.* Journal of Musculoskeletal and Neuronal Interactions, 7(4), 314-315.
- **Fitts, R. H., et al. (2010)**. *Prolonged space flight-induced alterations in the structure and function of human skeletal muscle fibres.* The Journal of Physiology, 588(18), 3567-3592.

## 3. Space Radiation & Biological Impact
- **Cucinotta, F. A., et al. (2013)**. *Space radiation risks to the central nervous system.* Life Sciences in Space Research, 1, 35-46.
- **NASA Space Radiation Program (2020)**. *Human Health and Performance Risks of Space Exploration Missions.* NASA SP-2009-3405.

## 4. Behavioral Health, Sleep Architecture, & Circadian Disruption
- **Basner, M., et al. (2013)**. *Mars 520-d mission simulation reveals increasing sleep duration and behavioral deficits in isolated environments.* PNAS, 110(7), 2632-2637.
- **Barger, L. K., et al. (2014)**. *Prevalence of sleep deficiency and use of hypnotic drugs in astronauts before, during, and after spaceflight: an observational study.* The Lancet Neurology, 13(9), 904-912.

## 5. Early Warning Algorithms & Biomedical Anomaly Detection
- **Chandola, V., Banerjee, A., & Kumar, V. (2009)**. *Anomaly detection: A survey.* ACM Computing Surveys, 41(3), 1-58.
- **NASA Technical Standard (NASA-STD-3001)**. *NASA Space Flight Human-System Standard, Volume 1: Crew Health.*
